# Resting Heart Rate: Adult Reference and Limits

For most adults, the American Heart Association (AHA) describes a resting heart rate of 60–100 beats per minute as typical. This is an adult reference range, not a target or a diagnosis. The AHA notes that physical activity, stress, anxiety, hormones and medicines can affect a person's rate; an athlete or more active person may have a lower resting rate.

The previous page included age-by-age values for children, teens and adults without an age-specific source. Those values and claims that “lower is better” have been removed. This page does not establish pediatric reference ranges.

The AHA defines resting heart rate as the number of beats per minute while at rest. A reading is one measurement; interpretation depends on context and symptoms. If you have concerns about your pulse or symptoms, consult a health professional rather than relying on this range alone.
