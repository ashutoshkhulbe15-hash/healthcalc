# Peanut Butter During Pregnancy

The [NIAID food-allergy prevention guidelines](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/faguidelinesexecsummary.pdf) do not recommend restricting a pregnant person's diet to prevent food allergy in a child. That statement does not mean eating peanut butter will prevent allergy, nor does it override an allergy the pregnant person already has.

Ingredients and allergen labeling vary by product. Check the package label for the specific product.

The NIAID recommendation addresses maternal diet and allergy prevention. It does not set a peanut-butter serving target or establish that any particular brand is safe for every person.

*Educational information. This page does not diagnose food allergy or recommend introducing an allergen to someone with an allergy.*

## Source

[NIAID: Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Management of Food Allergy, Executive Summary](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/faguidelinesexecsummary.pdf).
