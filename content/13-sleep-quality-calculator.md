# Sleep Duration and Efficiency Estimator

## Overnight sleep calculation
The tool assumes one sleep period shorter than 24 hours. Time in bed crosses midnight when wake time is earlier than bedtime. Subtract minutes to fall asleep and minutes awake during the night. Efficiency is estimated sleep divided by time in bed, multiplied by 100.

For 23:00 to 07:00, fifteen minutes to fall asleep and no further wakefulness, time in bed is eight hours and estimated sleep is 7.75 hours. Efficiency is approximately 96.9%. Equal bed and wake times are ambiguous and rejected.

## What the estimate cannot tell you
Recall may be inaccurate, and time arithmetic cannot determine whether sleep is restorative or diagnose a disorder. The previous shortened PSQI-style score has been withdrawn. The validated PSQI requires its complete questionnaire and scoring method; its research statistics do not apply to this diary estimator.
