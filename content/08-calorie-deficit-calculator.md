# Calorie Difference Calculator

This tool performs a subtraction using values you provide. Enter an estimated maintenance-energy value from a source you trust, then enter a separate comparison value. The result is arithmetic only; this page does not determine whether either number is accurate, appropriate, or safe for you.

## What the calculation means

The calculator subtracts the comparison amount from the maintenance estimate and reports the numerical difference. It does not estimate your energy needs, recommend a calorie intake, set a rate of weight change, or predict a goal date.

Energy needs and weight change are not captured reliably by a fixed calorie-to-weight conversion. A review by the U.S. National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases discusses how the familiar fixed energy-per-weight rule has limits and varies with body composition and the amount of weight lost ([Hall, 2008, PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17848938/)). For personalized planning, consult an appropriately qualified health professional or use a validated tool such as the [NIDDK Body Weight Planner](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/bwp).

This arithmetic tool is not designed for children or adolescents, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or people managing a medical condition or eating disorder. It does not replace individualized medical or nutrition advice.

## Calculation

`difference = entered maintenance estimate − entered comparison amount`

The result is a calculation from your inputs, not a clinical assessment or recommendation.

## Source

Hall KD. What is the required energy deficit per unit weight loss? *International Journal of Obesity.* 2008;32:573–576. [PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17848938/).
