# Burnout Guidance and Assessment Options

## Understanding the term
WHO describes burnout as an occupational phenomenon arising from unmanaged chronic workplace stress. Its description concerns exhaustion, distance or cynicism toward work, and reduced effectiveness. This is not a diagnosis produced by a website quiz.

## Assessments and support
Different instruments have specific questions, scoring methods and administration conditions. The previous adapted MBI-style questionnaire and arbitrary combined cutoffs have been withdrawn. For persistent problems, seek support and assessment that also considers other possible causes.
