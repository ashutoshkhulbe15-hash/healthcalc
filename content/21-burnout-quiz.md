# Burnout Guidance and Assessment Options

## Understanding the term
WHO classifies burnout in ICD-11 as an occupational phenomenon, not a medical condition. WHO defines it in the context of chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed, and describes exhaustion, mental distance or cynicism about one's job, and reduced professional efficacy. WHO says the term applies specifically to work and should not be used for other areas of life. [WHO definition](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-05-2019-burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon-international-classification-of-diseases).

## Assessments and support
Different instruments have their own wording, scoring methods and administration terms. The previous adapted MBI-style questionnaire and combined cutoffs on this site have been withdrawn. This page does not assess or diagnose burnout.
