# One-repetition maximum estimates

The calculator on this page is temporarily unavailable while its equation sources and population-specific accuracy are reviewed. Published prediction equations can differ, and a numerical estimate is not a measured lift.

The previous page also included unsupported accuracy margins, training-zone recommendations, safety claims, and advice about testing frequency; those claims have been removed. This page does not recommend a lifting load or exercise program.

For general information about resistance-training research, see the [American College of Sports Medicine's current position stands](https://acsm.org/education-resources/pronouncements-scientific-communications/position-stands/). The original Brzycki paper is linked here for reference: [Brzycki, *Strength testing: predicting a one-rep max from reps-to-fatigue*](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/07303084.1993.10606684). Its full source passage could not be directly checked for this review, so the site is not applying its formula at this time.
