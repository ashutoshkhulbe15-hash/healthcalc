# Pregnancy Food Guide: Reviewed Topics and Scope

This directory links to nine food topics with completed source review. It does not classify every food as safe or unsafe. Open an individual page for the cited source, population, regional scope and preparation details.

The reviewed topics are alcohol, coffee and caffeine, matcha and caffeine, eggs, peanut butter, salmon, cod, tuna and sushi. Other foods are not included in this searchable directory; this page makes no claim about them.
