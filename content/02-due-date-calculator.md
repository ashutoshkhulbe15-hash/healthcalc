# Due Date Calculator: Method, Examples and Limits

This tool gives an estimated due date from a last menstrual period, an assumed conception date or a clinic-confirmed day-3/day-5 embryo transfer. It also displays gestational weeks and days for a current pregnancy.

## Formula used

| Input method | Days added |
|---|---|
| First day of last menstrual period | 280 |
| Known or assumed fertilization date | 266 |
| Day-3 embryo transfer | 263 |
| Day-5 embryo transfer | 261 |

[ACOG Committee Opinion 700](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2017/05/methods-for-estimating-the-due-date) describes menstrual dating and embryo-age-based ART dating. First-trimester ultrasound is the most accurate ultrasound method to establish or confirm gestational age. A conception date inferred from intercourse is not necessarily a known fertilization date. Do not replace an established clinical due date from this calculator.

## Worked examples

A last period beginning January 1, 2026 gives October 8, 2026 when 280 calendar days are added. A day-5 transfer on March 15, 2026 gives December 1, 2026 after adding 261 days; a day-3 transfer that same date gives December 3.

The conception-date option adds 266 days, derived from the 280-day dating convention minus its assumed 14 days before fertilization. A date inferred from intercourse remains an assumption. The displayed gestational age is calculated from your input; it does not confirm pregnancy.

## What the estimate does not mean

The menstrual calculation assumes a regular 28-day cycle and does not adjust for an entered cycle length. Actual ovulation timing and uncertain menstrual dates limit its accuracy. This tool rejects future input dates for a current-pregnancy result and gestation beyond its supported 42-week display.

A due date does not predict the exact day of delivery. The trimester labels follow [ACOG’s pregnancy explanation](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/how-your-fetus-grows-during-pregnancy): the second begins at 14 weeks and the third at 28 weeks.

For other embryo ages or uncertain IVF dating, use your clinic's supplied date. The tool supports day 3 and day 5 only.

*Educational dating arithmetic. Your documented obstetric due date and care plan take precedence.*
