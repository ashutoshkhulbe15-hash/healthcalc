# Lean Body Mass Calculator

## Method and units
For male reference inputs, Boer lean mass in kg is 0.407 × weight in kg + 0.267 × height in cm − 19.2. For female inputs it is 0.252 × weight + 0.473 × height − 48.3.

James is a separate comparison: male 1.1 × weight − 128 × (weight / height in cm)²; female 1.07 × weight − 148 × (weight / height in cm)². The height in this squared term is centimeters, not meters. We do not average these equations. Fat mass and estimated fat percentage use the Boer result.

## Worked example
An 80 kg, 180 cm male reference input gives Boer 61.42 kg and James approximately 62.72 kg. Estimated fat mass from Boer is 18.58 kg. This is an equation estimate, not a body-composition measurement.

## Limits
These historical adult equations do not fit every body type. Impossible estimates are rejected instead of being displayed. Do not use this for children, pregnancy, diagnosis or a medical nutrition prescription. No fixed individual error margin is promised.
