# Sushi During Pregnancy: Raw and Cooked Fish

ACOG advises avoiding raw or undercooked fish during pregnancy because it is more likely than fully cooked fish to contain parasites or bacteria. The advice applies to raw-fish sushi and sashimi. A menu label or “sushi-grade” wording does not tell you whether a particular preparation was cooked.

ACOG says many types of fish are safe when fully cooked. FDA's cooking guidance says finfish should reach 145°F (63°C), or be opaque and flaky when a thermometer is not available or appropriate. FDA/EPA mercury categories still apply to cooked fish: cooking does not change which fish category a species is in.

Sushi can contain several ingredients. Ask which fish and preparation are used; this page cannot verify restaurant handling, cross-contamination, refrigeration or the mercury category of an unspecified fish. Check the [FDA/EPA fish chart](https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/questions-answers-fdaepa-advice-about-eating-fish-those-who-might-become-or-are-pregnant-or) for species-specific advice.

*Educational information. This page does not certify a restaurant dish or product.*
