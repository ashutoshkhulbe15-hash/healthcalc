# Total daily energy expenditure estimate

This tool combines the Mifflin–St Jeor resting energy expenditure (REE) equation with a selected activity multiplier. The source equation was derived from 498 healthy adults aged 19–78 and predicts REE; it does not itself calculate total daily expenditure. [Mifflin et al., 1990, original study and equation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/).

## Calculation shown

For weight in kilograms, height in centimeters, and age in years, the equation is:

- Male: REE = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5
- Female: REE = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age − 161

The calculator multiplies this estimate by the selected factor (1.20, 1.375, 1.55, 1.725, or 1.90) to produce an illustrative TDEE estimate. These factors are site-selected model assumptions; they are not official activity categories or clinical recommendations. The output is arithmetic based on the inputs and should not be treated as measured energy expenditure or a personalized calorie target.

The current U.S. Dietary Guidelines say calorie needs depend on age, sex, height, weight, and physical activity. This calculator does not determine an individual's needs or recommend an intake for weight change. [U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2025–2030, p. 2](https://cdn.realfood.gov/DGA.pdf).

The original Mifflin–St Jeor equation study included healthy adults ages 19–78. The tool limits its inputs to this source age range; using an equation within that range still does not guarantee an accurate estimate for an individual.

## Method

1. Convert pounds to kilograms using 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg and inches to centimeters using 1 in = 2.54 cm when needed.
2. Calculate REE using the sex-specific Mifflin–St Jeor formula.
3. Multiply REE by the selected activity factor and round to the nearest kcal/day.
