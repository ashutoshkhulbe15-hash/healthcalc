# Pregnancy Weight Gain Calculator

## What this calculator shows
Enter height, pre-pregnancy weight and current weight in one unit system. BMI uses converted kilograms and meters. Gain so far is current minus pre-pregnancy weight. Switching units clears measurements so they are not reinterpreted.

The calculator displays CDC total-pregnancy ranges separately for one baby and twins. The underweight twin range has a different evidence source, noted in the result. Higher multiples need an individual plan. These totals are not targets for your current week; no weekly curve is inferred.

## How to use the result
Discuss your trend and personal circumstances with your maternity team. Weight change alone cannot assess pregnancy health. Seek clinical advice for concerning symptoms rather than relying on this calculator.

## Source
[CDC: pregnancy weight-gain recommendations, evidence and trackers](https://www.cdc.gov/maternal-infant-health/pregnancy-weight/index.html).
