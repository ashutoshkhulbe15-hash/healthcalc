# Resting energy estimate using Mifflin–St Jeor

The Mifflin–St Jeor equation predicts resting energy expenditure (REE), not basal metabolic rate measured under laboratory conditions. The original study derived the equation from 498 healthy adults, ages 19–78, and reports separate formulas for males and females. [Mifflin et al., 1990, original study and formula](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/).

## Formula

For weight in kilograms, height in centimeters, and age in years:

- Male: REE = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5
- Female: REE = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age − 161

For example, for a female aged 32 years, weighing 68 kg and 165 cm tall, the equation gives 10 × 68 + 6.25 × 165 − 5 × 32 − 161 = 1,390.25 kcal/day, displayed rounded to 1,390 kcal/day.

This is a prediction from a population equation, not a measurement, diagnosis, minimum calorie requirement, or personal intake recommendation. The study's age and health population matter; the calculator only accepts ages 19–78 and does not claim the equation is accurate for every person in that range.

The U.S. Dietary Guidelines state that calorie needs depend on age, sex, height, weight, and physical activity. This resting-energy estimate alone does not account for total daily activity or determine calorie needs. [U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2025–2030, p. 2](https://cdn.realfood.gov/DGA.pdf).
