# Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator: Measurement and Limits

Waist-to-hip ratio is waist circumference divided by hip circumference. Use the same units for both measurements. For a waist of 80 cm and hips of 100 cm, the ratio is 0.800; the units cancel.

## Choose a consistent measurement protocol

The WHO expert consultation describes waist circumference measured around the abdomen at the approximate midpoint between the lower margin of the last palpable rib and the top of the iliac crest. Hip circumference is measured around the maximum circumference over the buttocks. Stand relaxed and keep the tape horizontal; do not mix measurement sites between measurements. Protocols and cutoffs vary with sex, age and ethnicity.

## Interpreting the result

This calculator reports the arithmetic ratio without assigning an overall health-risk category. WHO discusses population-level associations and measurement protocols; a single ratio does not diagnose a condition or determine a person's cardiovascular risk. Age, population, measurement technique and other risk factors affect interpretation.
