# Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator: Measurement and Limits

Waist-to-hip ratio is waist circumference divided by hip circumference. Use the same units for both measurements. For a waist of 80 cm and hips of 100 cm, the ratio is 0.800.

## Consistent measurement matters

Different waist measurement sites can produce different results. Follow an agreed measurement protocol rather than mixing measurements taken at the navel, narrowest waist and other sites. Keep the tape level and avoid compressing the skin. Discuss an appropriate protocol with a healthcare professional.

## Interpreting the result

This tool reports the ratio without assigning an overall health risk category. Body-fat distribution measures are associated with population health outcomes, but a single ratio does not diagnose a condition or determine your personal cardiovascular risk. Age, sex, population, measurement technique and other risk factors affect interpretation.
