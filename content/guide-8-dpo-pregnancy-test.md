# 8 DPO Pregnancy Test — What an Early Result Means

## An early negative is not conclusive
A home pregnancy test detects hCG. At eight days after estimated ovulation, an early negative cannot reliably exclude pregnancy. The FDA explains that testing too early can give a negative result even when pregnancy is present. Estimated ovulation dates do not establish a guaranteed detection day. [FDA: early negatives and test accuracy](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/home-use-tests/pregnancy).

## Testing and repeat results
Follow the instructions for the exact test. If the result is negative and pregnancy is still possible, repeat it after several days or seek clinical advice. Testing around or after a missed period is more informative than an extremely early test. First-morning urine may help when testing early. [FDA: test instructions, first-morning urine and repeating a negative](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/home-use-tests/pregnancy).

## Lines do not measure a pregnancy's progress
Home urine tests provide a qualitative result, not a measured hCG concentration. Do not infer a quantitative hCG level or pregnancy progress from line shade or photo comparisons. [FDA: the test is qualitative](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/home-use-tests/pregnancy).
