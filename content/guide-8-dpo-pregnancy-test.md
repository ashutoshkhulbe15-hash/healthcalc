# 8 DPO Pregnancy Test — What an Early Result Means

## An early negative is not conclusive
A home pregnancy test detects hCG. At eight days after estimated ovulation, an early negative cannot reliably exclude pregnancy. Ovulation timing, implantation, urine concentration and the particular test affect what a result can show.

We have removed the unsupported universal “average hCG of 0.06” and precise daily progression table. A study population's distribution should not be presented as an individual's expected level.

## Testing and repeat results
Follow the instructions for the exact test. If the result is negative and pregnancy is still possible, repeat it after several days or seek clinical advice. Testing around or after a missed period is more informative than an extremely early test. First-morning urine may help when testing early.

## Lines do not measure a pregnancy's progress
Line darkness is not a calibrated hCG measurement and cannot establish viability or location. Do not rely on photo comparisons or brand-specific guarantees. Seek medical care for concerning symptoms, regardless of the result.
