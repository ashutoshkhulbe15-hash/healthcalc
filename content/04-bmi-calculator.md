# Adult BMI calculator

For adults age 20 and older, body mass index (BMI) is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. In U.S. CDC categories, BMI below 18.5 is underweight; 18.5 to less than 25 is healthy weight; 25 to less than 30 is overweight; and 30 or higher is obesity. The obesity category has classes 1, 2, and 3 at BMI 30–<35, 35–<40, and ≥40. [CDC: Adult BMI Categories](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/adult-calculator/bmi-categories.html)

For pounds and inches, the calculator uses the equivalent formula **BMI = weight (lb) × 703 ÷ height (in)²**. For example, 75 kg at 1.75 m gives BMI 24.5. These are arithmetic calculations; the displayed category is a screening category, not a diagnosis.

## Limits

CDC describes BMI as a screening measure to consider with other information about a person's health. BMI does not directly measure body fat and cannot distinguish fat, muscle and bone or show where body fat is carried. A higher BMI category is associated with increased risk for some health problems at a population level, but an individual's health cannot be determined from BMI alone. [CDC: About BMI](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/about/index.html); [CDC BMI FAQ](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/faq/)

This calculator is intended for adults age 20 and older. CDC uses age- and sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles for children and teens ages 2–19; use a child/teen method for that population. [CDC: BMI categories](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/adult-calculator/bmi-categories.html); [CDC: Child and Teen BMI Categories](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/child-teen-calculator/bmi-categories.html)
