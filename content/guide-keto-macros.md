# Keto Macro Calculator: What the Numbers Represent

There is no universally established macro ratio or carbohydrate amount that this page can promise will cause or maintain ketosis for an individual. The sources on this page do not support a universal macro prescription for everyone. The previous fixed carb limits, macro percentages, protein prescriptions, “keto flu” electrolyte doses and symptom claims have therefore been removed.

## Macro arithmetic

The calculator distributes an entered energy estimate among carbohydrate, protein and fat. Its result is arithmetic based on the selected preset and estimated energy input; it does not measure energy needs, ketones, nutritional adequacy or clinical suitability. A preset output should not be read as a treatment plan or a personal intake recommendation.

For arithmetic only, this page uses the conventional Atwater conversion factors: approximately 4 kcal per gram of carbohydrate, 4 kcal per gram of protein and 9 kcal per gram of fat. Example: 100 g carbohydrate, 100 g protein and 100 g fat correspond to approximately 1,700 kcal by that arithmetic (400 + 400 + 900). This is not a ketogenic target.

Research reviews describe ketogenic diets as studied interventions with varied protocols and potential clinical uses; those reviews do not establish one universal macro prescription for everyone. People considering a restrictive diet for a health condition should discuss that decision with a qualified health professional.

## Sources
