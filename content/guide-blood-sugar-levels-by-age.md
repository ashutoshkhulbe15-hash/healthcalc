# Blood Sugar Levels by Age — Tests and Targets

## Diagnosis and treatment targets answer different questions
Age alone does not define a universal glucose target. Diagnostic thresholds concern specified laboratory tests. Treatment goals for someone with diabetes are individualized and can differ with pregnancy, health, medication and hypoglycemia risk. The earlier table combined these concepts and has been removed.

## Nonpregnant diagnostic tests
| Test | Prediabetes range | Diabetes threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Fasting plasma glucose | 100–125 mg/dL | 126 mg/dL or more |
| Two-hour plasma glucose after a 75 g OGTT | 140–199 mg/dL | 200 mg/dL or more |
| A1C | 5.7–6.4% | 6.5% or more |

These are laboratory criteria, not a diagnosis from one home-meter reading. Confirmation and clinical context are needed except in the circumstances specified by the guideline. An ordinary meal is not a standardized OGTT.

## Pregnancy uses a different protocol
In the one-step 75 g gestational-diabetes OGTT protocol, thresholds are fasting 92, one-hour 180 and two-hour 153 mg/dL. The 153 value belongs to two hours, not one hour. Other protocols exist; follow your maternity team's test instructions and interpretation.
