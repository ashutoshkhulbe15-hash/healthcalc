# Thyroid Lab Reference Range Comparison

## Use your own lab intervals
Enter the value and lower/upper limits from the same report, with matching units. Free T4 is optional, but a value needs both reference limits. The output states below, within or above the supplied interval. It does not assign a thyroid diagnosis or treatment.

## Clinical interpretation
A TSH within a lab interval does not settle every thyroid question. Free T4, symptoms, medications and other circumstances can change interpretation. Pregnancy needs appropriate lab and trimester reference intervals and clinician review. Discuss abnormal or discordant results with your clinician.
