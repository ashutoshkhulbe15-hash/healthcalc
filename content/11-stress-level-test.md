# Perceived Stress Scale — PSS-10

## What the score means
PSS-10 asks about perceived stress over the past month. Displayed choices receive zero to four points. Items four, five, seven and eight are reverse-scored, and the ten values sum to zero to forty.

Higher totals reflect more perceived stress. The instrument has no diagnostic score cutoffs. The previous low/moderate/high bands were not clinical categories and have been removed. Compare concerns in context rather than treating a score as a diagnosis.

## Source and support
[Cohen laboratory at Carnegie Mellon: instruments, scoring and limitations](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/psychology/stress-immunity-disease-lab/scales/index.html).

Discuss persistent distress or difficulties with a healthcare professional. A low score does not remove the need for support when you are concerned.
