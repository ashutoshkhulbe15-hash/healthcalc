# Understanding Fetal Weight Percentiles

## Why the reference matters
Estimated fetal weight is derived from ultrasound measurements. A percentile additionally requires precise gestational age and a specified reference. Combining an approximate table with the names of different standards does not establish a valid percentile.

## What to ask your maternity team
Ask which reference was used, what gestational age was entered, and how the result compares with earlier scans and the rest of the assessment. A low or high weight percentile alone does not establish a diagnosis. Do not change monitoring or treatment based on a homemade estimate.

The earlier percentile calculation has been withdrawn while a single reference and its implementation can be established and reviewed.
