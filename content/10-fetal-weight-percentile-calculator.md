# Understanding Fetal Weight Percentiles

## Why the reference matters
Estimated fetal weight is derived from ultrasound measurements. A percentile additionally requires precise gestational age and a specified reference. Combining an approximate table with the names of different standards does not establish a valid percentile.

## What to ask your maternity team
Ask which reference was used, what gestational age was entered, and how the result compares with earlier scans and the rest of the assessment. [SMFM defines fetal growth restriction using estimated fetal weight or abdominal circumference below the 10th percentile for gestational age, and recommends a population-based reference such as Hadlock](https://publications.smfm.org/publications/289-society-for-maternal-fetal-medicine-consult-series-52/). This website does not calculate or diagnose fetal growth restriction, and a homemade estimate should not guide monitoring or treatment.

This guide does not calculate a percentile from a weight value. Use the percentile and reference reported by your maternity team.
