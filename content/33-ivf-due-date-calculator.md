# IVF Due Date Calculator: Day-3 and Day-5 Transfer Dating

Enter the embryo transfer date and the day-3 or day-5 age confirmed by your clinic. The calculator estimates the due date and current gestational age. Fresh/frozen selection does not change its arithmetic.

## Embryo age changes the offset

The calculation is **266 − embryo age in days** after transfer: 263 days for day 3 and 261 for day 5. These are the examples specified in [ACOG Committee Opinion 700](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2017/05/methods-for-estimating-the-due-date).

For a March 15, 2026 transfer:

- Day 5: March 15 + 261 days = December 1, 2026.
- Day 3: March 15 + 263 days = December 3, 2026.

The equivalent menstrual starting date is transfer minus 19 days for a day-5 embryo or minus 17 for day 3. Those offsets include the conventional two weeks before fertilization in gestational dating.

## Limits

This tool supports day-3 and day-5 transfers only. Do not substitute one of those options for another embryo age. It rejects future transfer dates and pregnancies outside its current-gestation display. Use the established date and follow-up schedule supplied by your fertility or maternity team.

See the [general due-date calculator](/pregnancy/due-date-calculator) for other supported input methods. Its IVF option uses the same embryo-age offsets.

*Educational arithmetic. An IVF due date is a dating estimate, not a prediction of delivery or a statement about pregnancy risk.*
