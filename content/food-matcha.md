# Matcha and Caffeine During Pregnancy

Matcha is a powdered form of green tea, as described in this [peer-reviewed chemical-composition review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7796401/). The amount of caffeine in a serving depends on the product and how much powder is used; a café drink or recipe may not use a standard amount. Check the product or ask the preparer when possible, and include its caffeine in your total for the day.

[ACOG advises keeping total caffeine intake below 200 mg per day during pregnancy](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy). That guidance covers caffeine overall. It does not establish a matcha-specific serving limit.

## Matcha-specific limits

This page does not set a matcha-specific serving limit, recommend a supplement schedule, or assess concentrated green-tea products. The cited ACOG guidance concerns total caffeine, not a specific matcha product.

For general caffeine guidance, see the [coffee page](/pregnancy/safe-food/coffee). A product name or serving size alone cannot confirm its caffeine content.

*Educational information. This article does not establish matcha as safe at a particular dose or assess a specific product.*
