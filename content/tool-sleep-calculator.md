# Sleep Calculator: Plan a Bedtime From Your Wake Time

Enter a wake time, your desired hours asleep and the minutes you expect to need to fall asleep. The planner subtracts sleep duration and sleep-onset time from your wake time, wrapping across midnight.

For a 07:00 wake time, eight hours asleep and 15 minutes to fall asleep, the planned bedtime is 22:45 on the previous evening. Nighttime awakenings are not included; use the [sleep diary estimator](/mental-health/sleep-quality-calculator) to estimate actual duration and efficiency.

## Why fixed cycle timing is not used

Sleep cycles vary. NHLBI describes cycles recurring about every 80–100 minutes, rather than a fixed 90 minutes. Clock arithmetic cannot identify which stage you will be in at your alarm. This planner does not promise to prevent grogginess or justify shorter sleep.

## Choosing a duration

Consult the CDC's age-specific recommendations. Persistent sleep difficulty or daytime sleepiness deserves professional assessment even when your calculated sleep duration appears sufficient.
