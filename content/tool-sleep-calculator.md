# Sleep Calculator: Plan a Bedtime From Your Wake Time

Enter a wake time, desired hours asleep and the minutes you expect to need to fall asleep. The planner subtracts the sleep duration and sleep-onset estimate from your wake time, wrapping across midnight. For a 07:00 wake time, eight hours asleep and 15 minutes to fall asleep, the planned bedtime is 22:45 on the previous evening. It does not include time awake during the night.

## Why fixed cycle timing is not used

NHLBI describes sleep cycles as recurring about every 80–100 minutes. Clock arithmetic cannot identify which stage you will be in at your alarm. This planner does not promise to prevent grogginess or justify shortening sleep.

## Choosing a duration

Sleep-duration recommendations vary by age. CDC recommends 7 or more hours for adults ages 18–60, 7–9 hours for ages 61–64, and 7–8 hours for people 65 and older. CDC lists different ranges for children and teens. These are population recommendations; use the range that applies to your age and circumstances. CDC advises talking to a healthcare provider for regular sleep problems or signs of a sleep disorder.
