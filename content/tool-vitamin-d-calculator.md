# Vitamin D Daily Intake Reference

## Recommended intake versus supplement dose
The NIH fact sheet lists 600 IU daily from age one through seventy and 800 IU from age seventy-one. These are recommended total intakes, not amounts to automatically add as supplements. This tool excludes infants.

## Individual care
A dose for deficiency or a medical condition requires clinical guidance. Weight, skin tone and a short sun-exposure question do not establish a treatment plan. The tool no longer assigns a universal blood target or a 4,000 IU repletion dose.
