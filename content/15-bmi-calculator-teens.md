# BMI Calculator for Children and Teens

## How age and sex are used
Enter completed years plus additional completed months. The tool uses the CDC half-month reference row for that completed-month interval, and the male or female reference specified. It covers ages two to under twenty years.

BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. CDC LMS parameters transform BMI into a z-score, then a standard-normal percentile. The original CDC 2000 dataset is bundled with the tool; invented medians and linear percentile guesses are not used.

## Reading the result
CDC categories use below the fifth percentile, fifth to below 85th, 85th to below 95th, and 95th or above. Classification uses unrounded values. BMI is a screen, not a diagnosis or an instruction to restrict a child's food.

Standard LMS estimates become unreliable in the tails. This tool suppresses numeric percentiles beyond z-scores ±3 and directs users to clinical assessment and extended charts where appropriate. Growth over time, medical history and development matter.
