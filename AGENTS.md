# ProHealthIt editorial requirements

These requirements record the website owner's explicit instruction from October 2, 2026 and apply to all future work in this project, including the source/healthcalc-main website.

- Publish no unverified medical, nutrition, health, food-safety or diagnostic claims. Remove or withhold a claim if its evidence cannot be verified.
- Support substantive health claims and numerical recommendations with an exact, directly relevant source from a major credible authority: government health agencies (e.g. NIH, CDC, FDA, USDA, NHS), recognized professional guideline bodies (e.g. ACOG, ASRM, NICE), National Academies, or a major academic medical institution. Do not treat search rankings, a citation list, a disclaimer, or another calculator website as verification.
- Read the supporting source, check the specific passage/table, units, population, country, exceptions and current guidance. A source's reputation alone does not establish that it supports a sentence. If direct access is unavailable, record the limitation and verify the precise statement through the authority's indexed text or another directly accessible authoritative source; omit it if that is insufficient.
- Put relevant source links next to claims on each page. Identify regional differences; do not combine conflicting advice into a universal rule.
- Distinguish reference intakes from supplement/treatment doses, observations from diagnoses, population guidance from personal targets, and arithmetic examples/model assumptions from clinical evidence. Calculator formulas must be traceable and tested; derived arithmetic must be labelled and must not imply clinical validation.
- Check the entire page, including calculator/result text, metadata, quick answers, FAQs, charts and shared cards. A disclaimer does not justify retaining an unsupported statement.
- Keep the existing disclaimers. Do not invent a medical reviewer, credentials, endorsements, practitioner approval, guarantees of safety, or guarantees against legal liability. The owner currently authorizes source-based review and does not require obtaining practitioner review.
- Maintain a page-by-page source/claim record and explicit reviewed/unreviewed scope. Never describe unreviewed pages or the whole site as verified because a limited batch passed review.
