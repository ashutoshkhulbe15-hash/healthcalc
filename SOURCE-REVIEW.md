# Source-based content review — pregnancy batch 1

This file preserves the historical batch 1 findings from October 2, 2026. Its counts and deployment notes describe that batch at the time and are superseded by the October 3 status below. The review remains source-based editorial work, not practitioner review.

## Findings corrected

| Pages | Finding and action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| General due date and IVF tools | Both implementations added two days too many for IVF. Corrected day-5 transfer to +261 days, day-3 to +263. Both use one tested helper. Removed inaccurate delivery-accuracy promises, day-5/day-6 conflation, universal IVF visit schedules and the claim that the menstrual input adjusts cycle length. | ACOG Committee Opinion 700, ART paragraph. |
| hCG tool and weekly guide | Removed a universal 72-hour normal/abnormal classifier, flat-result infinity, date-only timing and untraceable DPO/twin charts. The tool uses actual elapsed hours and reports change, doubling or halving without a pregnancy diagnosis. The weekly guide names a particular reference source instead of implying universal healthy ranges. | NICE NG126, section 1.8; Barnhart et al. 2016; MedlinePlus quantitative hCG reference; NHS ectopic symptom guidance. |
| Ovulation tool | Corrected calendar day counting and the seven-cell display. Six inclusive dates now match the stated ASRM calendar assumption. Removed a fixed prediction-error claim and individual pregnancy probabilities. Added an explicit contraception limitation. | ASRM 2022 committee opinion, fertile-window and calendar-method sections. |
| Spotting guide | Removed unsupported prevalence, colour/day rules that purported to distinguish ovulation from implantation, and reassurance that bleeding never needs treatment. Replaced with observation guidance and sourced assessment/symptom advice. | NHS bleeding-between-periods guidance and ectopic symptoms. |
| Prodromal-labor guide | Removed home inference of cervical dilation, unsupported duration promises and reliance on a timing threshold as the condition for seeking help. Explained the timer's limits and sourced situations requiring contact. | NHS signs of labor and urgent-contact guidance. |
| Weight-gain science and trimester guide | Removed precise pounds-per-body-part breakdown and blanket reassurance about early loss. Linked original rate ranges and their first-trimester assumption, kept singleton totals separate from twins, and explained what scale readings cannot establish. | National Academies 2009, Summary Table S-1 and surrounding scope; CDC gain recommendations and maternal warning signs. |
| Pregnancy nutrition | Corrected folate units and neural-tube timing, calcium age differences, total-intake versus supplement dosing, and the promise that every prenatal supplies all nutrients. Removed universal DHA treatment-like instructions. | NIH ODS pregnancy and folate fact sheets; ACOG energy guidance; FDA pregnancy food safety. |
| Steak and meat/deli guide | Corrected attribution of 160°F as the required minimum for every whole cut. Separated 145°F + three-minute rest for whole cuts from 160°F for ground meat and 165°F for poultry. Removed fixed microwave-duration guarantees and crispy/colour-only tests. | USDA consumer minimum-temperature chart; FDA pregnancy cooking guidance; CDC Listeria prevention. |
| Soft cheese and blue cheese | Removed “pasteurized means all soft cheese is safe cold.” Explained queso-fresco-type advice and NHS mould-ripened/blue categories, with source and regional scope. Removed the unsupported generic 158°F fallback. Updated the shared food checker's summary. | CDC Listeria prevention; NHS foods to avoid in pregnancy. |
| Pre-made salads | Distinguished premade deli salads from leafy products and removed a blanket classification of every salad. Explained ingredient and handling scope. | CDC Listeria prevention; FDA pregnancy food safety. |

All rewritten pages have their own title, description, social metadata and canonical. Old quick-answer boxes, FAQ content and injected diagrams on those pages were removed where they contradicted the revised evidence. Food-page breadcrumbs now point to the food guide. URLs are preserved.

## Source records

- [ACOG: Methods for Estimating the Due Date, Committee Opinion 700](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2017/05/methods-for-estimating-the-due-date). Original professional guidance; embryo-transfer offsets verified from ACOG's indexed text where direct retrieval was restricted.
- [ACOG: Obstetric terminology](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/dictionary).
- [NICE NG126: Diagnosis of viable intrauterine and tubal ectopic pregnancy](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG126/chapter/diagnosis-of-viable-intrauterine-pregnancy-and-of-tubal-ectopic-pregnancy). Current guideline page, updated June 17, 2026; hCG section 1.8 checked.
- [Barnhart et al., 2016: Differences in Serum hCG Rise](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27500326/). Original observational research, specific symptomatic population; lower-percentile rise estimates are not universal diagnostic boundaries.
- [MedlinePlus: Quantitative hCG blood test](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003510.htm). Consumer reference, not an original cohort dataset; selected values are explicitly labelled source-specific examples.
- [ASRM: Optimizing natural fertility, committee opinion, 2022](https://www.asrm.org/practice-guidance/practice-committee-documents/optimizing-natural-fertility-a-committee-opinion-2021/). Calendar-model assumptions and limitations checked.
- [NHS: Bleeding between periods or after sex](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/vaginal-bleeding-between-periods-or-after-sex/).
- [NHS: Ectopic-pregnancy symptoms](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ectopic-pregnancy/symptoms/).
- [NHS: Signs labor has begun](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/labour-and-birth/signs-that-labour-has-begun/).
- [National Academies: Weight Gain During Pregnancy, 2009, Summary](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/12584/chapter/2). Original Table S-1 and its assumptions checked.
- [CDC: Pregnancy weight gain](https://www.cdc.gov/maternal-infant-health/pregnancy-weight/index.html).
- [CDC: Urgent maternal warning signs](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html).
- [NIH ODS: Pregnancy nutrition reference](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Pregnancy-HealthProfessional/).
- [NIH ODS: Folate reference](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Folate-HealthProfessional/).
- [ACOG: Pregnancy energy guidance](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/experts-and-stories/ask-acog/how-much-weight-should-i-gain-during-pregnancy).
- [FDA: Pregnancy food safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/food-safety-pregnant-women-and-their-unborn-babies).
- [FDA: Cooking for pregnancy](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/cooking-food-safety-moms-be).
- [USDA FSIS: Safe minimum-temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart).
- [CDC: Listeria prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/listeria/prevention/index.html).
- [NHS: Foods to avoid in pregnancy](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/).

These links are included next to the relevant statements in the revised articles. Guideline, consumer summary and original research are distinguished rather than calling every source primary research. Site disclaimers remain. No reviewer identity or medical approval has been invented.
