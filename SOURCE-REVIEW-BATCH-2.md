# Source-based content review — pregnancy food and drink batch 2

Reviewed October 3, 2026. This batch covers 12 article pages, the related food directory and checker, and the shared labels/cards/metadata that surface them. The first batch covered 15 pregnancy pages, so 27 of the site’s 76 articles have now received this deeper page-by-page review; 49 articles remain unreviewed under this standard. The site as a whole is not verified.

## Page-by-page evidence decisions

| Page | Claims retained and checked | Scope, exclusions and source |
|---|---|---|
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/alcohol` | CDC says no known safe amount or time; its advice applies to beer, wine and spirits; stop after learning of pregnancy, attend checkups and discuss exposure; seek help if unable to stop. CDC describes associations, not a prediction for an individual exposure. | CDC Alcohol and Pregnancy, reviewed Aug 14, 2026. Claims and source page checked directly. |
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/coffee` | ACOG’s under-200-mg/day guidance; caffeine from all sources; approximate 12-oz coffee comparison; CO 462 wording on miscarriage/preterm birth and uncertainty about growth restriction; an ACOG medication example. | ACOG CO 462 and patient FAQ/medicine example. ACOG’s professional page was verified from its indexed full text; it describes evidence and does not promise zero risk. |
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/matcha` | Matcha is powdered green tea; count its caffeine toward the ACOG total; no matcha-specific dose or supplement schedule is set here. | Peer-reviewed composition review supports the descriptive tea statement only; ACOG CO 462 supports general caffeine guidance only. No pregnancy-specific EGCG threshold or matcha dose is asserted. |
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/eggs` | FDA advice for firm yolks/whites, 160°F egg casseroles/dishes, treated/pasteurized eggs for recipes served raw or undercooked, refrigeration and handling. UK NHS exception is explicitly limited to qualifying British Lion/Laid in Britain eggs within date. | FDA egg handling page and NHS pregnancy food page checked. US FDA advice is separated from the UK-specific exception. |
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/peanut-butter` | NIAID guideline 36 does not recommend restricting maternal diet during pregnancy/lactation to prevent food allergy. No serving target, individual allergy advice or prevention effect claimed. |
| `/pregnancy/safe-food/salmon` | FDA/EPA lists salmon as Best Choice; 2–3 weekly 4-oz servings are the combined Best Choices total; finfish cooking guidance; raw/undercooked fish guidance. | FDA/EPA fish FAQ/chart, FDA cooking page and ACOG sushi advice. Fish category is mercury advice, not a certification of a product or preparation. |

| `/pregnancy/safe-food/tuna` | FDA/EPA categories and amounts checked: canned light Best Choice, albacore and yellowfin Good Choices (one serving and no other fish that week), bigeye Choices to Avoid. | FDA/EPA chart and Q&A checked. | `/pregnancy/safe-food/sushi` | ACOG advises avoiding raw/undercooked fish; FDA cooking advice; FDA/EPA mercury categories remain applicable to cooked fish. | ACOG sushi advice, FDA cooking page and FDA/EPA fish advice. | `/guides/fish-seafood-pregnancy` | FDA/EPA adult categories, serving sizes, species examples and local-catch fallback; FDA cooking; ACOG raw-fish advice. | FDA/EPA, FDA cooking and ACOG sushi pages. Summary directs readers to the full chart; only examples are listed. |

The `/pregnancy/safe-food` hub now calls itself food guidance and explains that a listing is not a universal product or serving safety rating. The interactive checker no longer assigns safety labels or makes claims for 25 assorted foods; it is a searchable directory of the nine reviewed food articles. Its page text, menu/search names and metadata state that limited scope. The preserved disclaimer remains in the shared page shell.

## Sources read and exact use

- [CDC: About Alcohol Use During Pregnancy](https://www.cdc.gov/alcohol-pregnancy/about/index.html): no known safe amount/time; what to do after learning; reviewed date August 14, 2026.
- [ACOG: Moderate Caffeine Consumption During Pregnancy, CO 462](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy): less than 200 mg/day does not appear to be a major contributing factor in miscarriage or preterm birth; fetal growth restriction relationship remains undetermined. Verified using ACOG’s indexed full text when its page could not be opened in the browser.
- [ACOG: How much coffee can I drink while pregnant?](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/experts-and-stories/ask-acog/how-much-coffee-can-i-drink-while-pregnant): approximate 12-oz cup comparison and caffeine in other common products.
- [ACOG: Headaches and Pregnancy](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/headaches-and-pregnancy): example of a caffeine-containing medicine.
- [Review: Health Benefits and Chemical Composition of Matcha Green Tea](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7796401/): supports the description of matcha as powdered green tea only; not pregnancy guidance.
- [FDA: What You Need to Know About Egg Safety](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/what-you-need-to-know-about-egg-safety): firm yolks/whites, 160°F egg dishes, pasteurized eggs/products for raw recipes, refrigeration and handling.
- [NHS: Foods to Avoid in Pregnancy](https://www.nhs.uk/pregnancy/keeping-well/foods-to-avoid/): qualifying UK egg schemes and conditions.
- [NIAID: Food Allergy Guidelines Executive Summary, guideline 36](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/faguidelinesexecsummary.pdf): maternal diet restriction is not recommended as an allergy-prevention strategy. Verified in the official indexed PDF text.
- [FDA/EPA: Advice About Eating Fish](https://www.fda.gov/food/consumers/questions-answers-fdaepa-advice-about-eating-fish-those-who-might-become-or-are-pregnant-or): species categories, 4-oz adult serving, weekly rules, canned light/albacore/yellowfin/bigeye tuna, and local-catch fallback.
- [FDA: Cooking (Food Safety for Moms-to-Be)](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/cooking-food-safety-moms-be): finfish and other pregnancy cooking instructions.
- [ACOG: Can I Eat Sushi While I’m Pregnant?](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/experts-and-stories/ask-acog/can-i-eat-sushi-while-im-pregnant): raw/undercooked fish distinction and cooked-fish context.
- [CDC: Safer Food Choices for Pregnant Women](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html): riskier/safer food table, including sprouts, juice, dairy, eggs and seafood.
- [FDA: Cleaning (Food Safety for Moms-to-Be)](https://www.fda.gov/food/people-risk-foodborne-illness/cleaning-food-safety-moms-be): wash produce under running water; clean hands/surfaces.

The updated articles put links next to substantive advice and list sources at the end. Unsupported food claims, charts and quick-answer text were removed rather than retained under the disclaimer. This is source-based editorial review, not practitioner review, a diagnosis or a guarantee about legal exposure. The 49 articles outside this completed review have not been represented as verified.
